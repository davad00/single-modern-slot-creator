## Originality review

- **Scatter-pays + tumble + orb multiplier** is a market convention (Pragmatic-class); the
  *mechanics* are generic and safe under original names; the *presentation identities* (candy
  gradients, god-portrait side character, orb-lightning trade dress) are not — keep distance [R2][R4].
- **Potion/apothecary theming** exists only on 7×7 cluster archetypes (W1, W2) — the theme×archetype
  pairing "dark apothecary × 6×5 scatter-pays" has **no incumbent**.
- **Single-tier FS entry** dominates the niche (W2: 4sc → one mode). Our mandated 3/4/5+ materially
  distinct tiers is itself a differentiator validated by the Hacksaw multi-mode pattern [R2 §3].

**Differentiation requirements for step 3 (binding):**
1. 6×5 scatter-pays-anywhere archetype — NOT 7×7 cluster (distance from both potion incumbents).
2. Dark jewel poison palette + Victorian brass; never candy-bright gradients, no deity/mascot
   side-portrait composition, no anthropomorphic potion mascots.
3. The multiplier fantasy must be diegetic and original: essences distilled into the parlour's
   master vial (persistent multiplier), tier-exclusive prism doubler — not thrown/struck orbs.
4. Three structurally different tiers (start multiplier, orb economy, exclusive doubler symbol,
   environment per tier) — not one mode scaled by spin count.
5. Adult framing throughout: poison-noir perfumery; zero confectionery cues; no child-appealing
   mascots (CAP-code adjacency, research/12 §4).

## Design opportunities

1. **Open pairing:** dark-apothecary theme × scatter-pays 6×5 — both niche incumbents are 7×7
   cluster [W1][W2]; the dominant package (C1) has no potion-themed occupant [R3].
2. **Tier hierarchy as trust + content depth** vs single-mode incumbents: per-tier disclosed RTP
   and materially distinct structures [R2 §3 multi-mode observation; R6 D3].
3. **Dark-fantasy palette differentiation** in bright lobbies — commercial growth cluster
   2024–26 [R2 §4].
4. **Transparency features** as marketing: published max-win odds + active-RTP display in-game
   (mandatory GB/MT/SE anyway) [R2 §3].
